# Acme Cloud – Frequently Asked Questions

## Account and sign-in

### I didn't receive the verification email. What should I do?
Check your spam or promotions folder. Verification links expire after 24 hours; you can request a new one from the sign-in page with **Resend verification email**.

### Why is my account locked?
After five failed sign-in attempts, the account is locked for 15 minutes. Wait, then try again or reset your password.

### I received a password reset email I did not request. Is my account at risk?
Someone may have typed your email address by mistake, or someone may be trying to access your account. Your password has not changed unless you clicked the link. As a precaution, change your password, turn on two-factor authentication and review **Profile → Security → Active sessions**.

### How do I change the email address I sign in with?
Go to **Profile → Account → Email**, enter the new address and confirm it from the email we send.

## Billing

### What payment methods do you accept?
Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, American Express), UPI AutoPay and, for annual ENTERPRISE plans, bank transfer against an invoice.

### I was charged twice. What should I do?
Duplicate charges are usually a temporary authorisation that your bank releases within 5–7 working days. If both charges are still present after 7 working days, contact billing support with both transaction references; confirmed duplicate payments are refunded in full. See the Refund Policy.

### How do I change the billing email address?
Account owners can change it in **Settings → Billing → Billing contact**. Invoices are sent to this address.

### My GST number is wrong on an invoice. Can it be corrected?
Yes. Update your GSTIN in **Settings → Business details**, then contact billing support with the invoice numbers. We issue corrected invoices (a credit note and a new invoice) for the current and previous financial year.

### Do you offer discounts for NGOs or education?
Registered non-profits and educational institutions in India get 30% off the PRO plan. Contact sales with your registration certificate.

## Features

### Can I export my contacts to CSV?
Yes. Go to **Contacts**, select the contacts, and choose **Export → CSV**. Large exports are emailed to you.

### Can I export all invoices as a single PDF?
Not currently. You can download invoices individually or as a zip of PDFs via **Export → PDF (zip)**.

### Can reports be emailed automatically?
Yes, on PRO and ENTERPRISE plans. Open the report and click **Schedule**.

### Do you integrate with Tally?
Not yet. A Tally integration is on our roadmap but there is no release date. Today you can export invoices to Excel and import them into Tally, or use our Zoho Books and QuickBooks integrations.

### Is there a dark mode?
Yes, in the mobile app from version 3.3. Dark mode for the web dashboard is planned.

### Which languages are supported?
English, Hindi, Tamil, Marathi and Bengali.

## Support

### What are your support hours?
Email and chat support is available Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 21:00 IST. ENTERPRISE customers have 24×7 phone support for P1 incidents. During national holidays, including Diwali, support runs from 10:00 to 18:00 IST for chat and email; P1 phone support is unaffected.

### How do I check if Acme Cloud is down?
Visit status.acmecloud.example for live service status and incident updates.

### How do I contact support?
Chat from the **?** icon in the dashboard, or email support@acmecloud.example. Billing questions go to billing@acmecloud.example.
